What a browser can and cannot do, straight: these reminders are fired by this page, so they only arrive while this tab is open somewhere. A page you have closed cannot wake itself up to buzz you — that is a browser limit, not a paid upgrade. Leave the tab open or pin it and they work. The mid-day nudge also shows up in the page itself, so it still reaches you if you say no to notifications or your browser does not support them.
