How it works — the 4-step screen

Before the call. Send the intake questionnaire. How they fill it in tells you as much as what they write.
On the call. Run the discovery script; keep the 12 Red Flags open in another tab and tick anything you hear.
After the call. Score them on the scorecard. Sleep on anything in the amber band.
Decide on purpose. If it's a no, send the matching decline email. If it's a yes, drop in a deposit-and-terms opening line and move to your contract.
