Why it works with hard-to-include people

The age tiers are the whole trick. A child who cannot answer "what does the Declaration argue" can answer "what is the best thing about where we live," and both answers land in the same round. An elder who has stopped joining games is the one person in the room who can answer the Living History pages — so the kit gives them the seat of honour instead of a chair at the edge.
