Find the complaint. Build the fix.

Every product starts with evidence, not a hunch. Here's the whole pipeline.

01 — RESEARCH

Mine the complaints

We read hundreds of one- and two-star app-store and forum reviews per category and quote the exact words people use.

02 — RANK

Find the loudest signal

Complaints are ranked by frequency and severity. A one-off gripe doesn't ship a feature; a pattern across hundreds of reviews does.

03 — BUILD

Build only the fix

No feature bloat. Each build solves the top complaints and nothing else, with a written self-check so the promise is a test, not a tagline.

04 — SHIP

Free or freemium, and gated for review

Every build goes through a security scan and a human approval gate before it ever reaches a user.

Our standard

Rules we don't bend, because a company that fixes broken software can't ship broken software.

  • Every feature traces to a quoted, real complaint — never a guess
  • No feature ships without a written, runnable self-check
  • No fake benchmarks, no claim we haven't verified ourselves
  • We solve the complaint — we don't clone the product
  • Security-scanned before anything reaches a user
  • Every release goes through a human approval gate before it's public
