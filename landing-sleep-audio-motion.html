Questions people actually ask

Plain answers, including the ones that are bad for us.

Is It Bad to Listen to Rain Sounds Every Night?

Nightly use is a comfort choice, not a risk you have to manage, as long as the level stays low and comfortable. The honest caveat: steady sound suits some people and irritates others, and a few sleep worse with it. A persistent sleep problem is a conversation for a clinician, not a speaker.

Brown Noise vs White Noise for Sleep: Which Should You Choose?

White noise spreads equal energy across every frequency, so it reads as bright hiss. Brown noise falls away about 6 dB per octave as pitch rises, which is why it sounds like distant rainfall or a jet cabin. If white feels sharp or glaring to you, brown is the gentler one. Dusk sells brown; we do not sell white.

Best Rain Sound Volume for Sleeping

Low enough to disappear, loud enough to cover what wakes you — usually a little above the noise you are masking and no higher. Every Dusk master is loudness-normalised so it cannot creep up while you drift off: Deep Brown measures a mean −18.6 dBFS and Pink Noise −20.8 dBFS, checked with ffprobe.

Can Brown Noise Help You Stay Asleep Through the Night?

Masking narrows the gap between the background level and a sudden noise, so a door slamming stands out less against it. That is a physical effect on the sound in your room, not a claim about your sleep. Whether it keeps you asleep is personal, and we will not pretend otherwise.

Can You Get Addicted to Sleeping With Rain Sounds?

Not in any clinical sense. What people usually mean is habit: the bedroom sounds different without it, so the first quiet night feels odd, and that fades. If you would rather not lean on it, use it only on the nights you want it — nothing in a sound file requires nightly use.

Do Rain Sounds Actually Block Out Noise?

Not block — mask. No audio removes a neighbour's television; it raises the steady floor so intermittent sounds are less prominent against it. Low, dull textures like brown noise and fan hum do this best against speech and thumps through a wall. Sharp, close sounds will still come through.

Rain Sounds for Sleep: Free vs Paid Options

Free versions are everywhere and plenty of them are fine. What you pay for is the absence of things: no ad break at 2am, no volume creep, no audible loop click, and a stated duration that is the measured one. Dusk masters are one-off downloads — no subscription, no account, no app.

How Long Does It Take Sleep Sounds to Actually Work?

The masking is there the moment the sound is on. Getting used to sleeping with a steady background usually takes a few nights. If a texture still irritates you after a week, it is the wrong texture for you — try a different one rather than a louder one.
