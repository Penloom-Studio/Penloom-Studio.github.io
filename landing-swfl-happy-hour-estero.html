Old Southwest Florida

This coast has always known how to end a day

Long before the first drink special, this coast gathered at the water when the workday ended — off the bridge, on the sand, at the races. The happy hour is just the modern version of an old SWFL ritual.

Arriving in Naples since 1927.Orange Blossom Special · State Archives of Florida
