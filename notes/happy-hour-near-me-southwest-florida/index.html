Happy Hour Near Me: A Southwest Florida Neighborhood Guide

It is a quarter past four somewhere between Naples and Cape Coral, and you want to know one thing: what is running right now, near here. Most guides answer with "4 to 6 PM" and a list scraped in 2022. On this coast, that is wrong more often than it is right.

Here is what the windows actually look like, neighborhood by neighborhood. Every venue and window below comes from the venue's own page or menu and was checked on 7 August 2026. Where a place does not publish its prices, this page says so instead of guessing.

SWFL has four happy hours, not one

Search "happy hour near me" anywhere else and you get one shape of deal. Here you get four, and picking the right one is most of the game:

  • The regular window. Usually somewhere in 3–7 PM, often bar-and-high-tops only — the detail that ruins the evening when you sit at a table.
  • The reverse happy hour. A second, late window — 8 PM, 10 PM, sometimes to 2 AM. Almost nobody lists these, and in season they are the only seats worth having.
  • Early dining. A fixed-price multi-course dinner, typically before 5:30 or 6 PM. Not a drink deal at all, and often the best value on the coast.
  • The all-day. A few places run discounted pricing from late morning to early evening, which makes "when does happy hour start" the wrong question.

A "happy hour near me" search only surfaces the first one. The other three are where the value hides.

Downtown Naples and Fifth Avenue South

The densest walkable cluster on the coast, and where the bar-only rule bites hardest.

  • Aqua on Fifth — daily, 4:00–7:00 p.m. $7 glasses of wine, $5–$6 domestic and imported beers, $7 top-shelf drinks, plus a happy hour food menu with raw bar items, steaks and small platters.
  • Vergina — daily, 3:00–5:00 p.m. 50% off the food menu, plus house martinis, well cocktails, mules, beers and select wines. An early window, so this is the one you miss by arriving at six.
  • Pazzo! Cucina Italiana — nightly, 4:30–7:00 p.m., at the bar. $12 Italian craft cocktails, $5 beers, $8–$10 wines by the glass, and 15% off all appetizers.
  • Foxboro Sports Tavern — daily, 11:00 a.m.–7:00 p.m. An eight-hour window: 2-for-1 house mixed cocktails, house wine and call drinks, plus $1 off draft and bottled beer.
  • Barbatella — daily 4:00–6:00 p.m., plus Wednesday from 4 p.m. to close. A three-course dinner special for $29, which is the early-dining play on Third Street South.

For the late crowd: K.C.'s Parrot 41 runs a genuine second happy hour every night from 10:00 p.m. to 2:00 a.m., on top of its 3:00–7:00 p.m. window.

  • Tap 42 Craft Kitchen & Bar — Monday–Friday, 3–6 p.m., bar only. $7 craft beers, $8 spirits, $8 wine, $8 shots, and $4 off all craft cocktails.
  • Violí — Monday–Thursday all day; Friday–Sunday 3:00–5:00 p.m. $5 beers, $9 wines and cocktails, $2 fresh oysters, $5 housemade dips and $8 small plates.
  • BURN by Rocky Patel — seven days a week, from opening at 2 p.m. until 7 p.m. 50% off beverages priced $14 and under, 25% off beverages priced $15 and over, with allocated and rare items excluded.

The late windows are the reason to know this neighborhood. Old Vines Naples at Mercato serves its regular cocktail menu from 10:00 p.m. to midnight Monday–Saturday and from 8:00 p.m. Sunday, and Taberna Burntwood runs a social hour in the bar and on the patio from 8:00 p.m. to close Monday–Friday, with half-off appetizers, $9 signature cocktails, $7 house wine, $5 draft and $4 bottled beer.

  • Mare Terra — daily, 4:00–10:00 p.m., bar and high-tops. Unusually long: house wines $6, domestic beer $5, imported beer $6, well drinks $7, signature drinks $12. Its "First Seating" three-course prix fixe runs Monday–Saturday, 4:00–5:30 p.m., at $26.26 per person.
  • North Bonita Country Club — daily, 11:00 a.m.–6:00 p.m. $6 house margaritas and $2.99 domestic drafts, with house and featured wines and well drinks varying slightly by day.
  • Lake House Bar & Grill — food specials 3:00–5:00 p.m., orders in by 5 p.m. sharp; drink discounts all day. 50% off all food with a beverage purchase, dine-in only, plus $2 off beers and $3 off cocktails.

How to use any happy hour list without getting burned

  • Check where, not just when. "Bar and high-tops only" appears in most of the listings above. Sitting at a table can quietly cost you the whole discount.
  • Watch the order cutoff. Several venues here need orders in before the window closes. Arriving at 5:58 for a 6:00 window is arriving late.
  • In season, assume it changed. This coast runs a summer schedule and a season schedule; a window accurate in March may not survive August.
  • Call before you drive. Which is why the directory records a source link and a check date on every listing.
Most happy hour guides are wrong because nobody ever goes back. A specials page that is never rechecked is a snapshot of a night that already happened.
