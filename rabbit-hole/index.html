Why the DISPUTED flag is the point

Most strange-history content online is strange precisely because the strangest part was invented. Strasbourg's death toll, Tunguska's object, Kowloon's population, the Emu War's kill counts — the viral version smooths all of that over. This is the version that survives checking, which means it tells you where the record stops.
